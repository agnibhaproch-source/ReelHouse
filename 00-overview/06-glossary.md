# 06 — Glossary

- **Row** — horizontal carousel with a title ("Trending now").
- **Billboard / Hero** — full-bleed featured movie at the top of home.
- **Card** — poster tile: idle, hover-preview, pressed, focused, loading.
- **Preview expand** — hovered card grows into a mini detail panel.
- **Scene** — pinned scroll section with its own timeline.
- **Cursor modes** — `default`, `link`, `play`, `drag`, `text`, `hidden`, `loading`.
- **Spotlight** — radial light following the cursor over dark surfaces.
- **Motion prefs** — combined OS + in-app reduced-motion setting.
